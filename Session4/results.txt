2. GAUSSIAN BLUR - WHAT SIGMA CONTROLS
======================================================================
scipy.ndimage.gaussian_filter(input, sigma): sigma is the standard
deviation of the Gaussian, in PIXELS. It sets how far a pixel is
smeared into its neighbours. scipy builds a kernel of half-width
int(truncate*sigma + 0.5), truncate=4.0 by default, so the window is
2*int(4*sigma + 0.5)+1 pixels wide. Bigger sigma = wider kernel =
more blur, and the cost grows with sigma.
